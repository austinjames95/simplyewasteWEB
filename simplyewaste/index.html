A Simple Approach to Electronic Waste.

Take Action

50 million metric tons of E-Waste are produced every year. Instead of ending up in landfills, we give it a second chance.

Our mission is to reduce global E-Waste by refurbishing devices that still meet today’s technological standards. These devices are then given to underserved communities and other various groups in need.

What We Do

Collect It

Be it law firms, IT companies, schools, or households, SimplyEwaste collects unused computers and gadgets that would have otherwise been sent to the dump. Public donation drives are often held for individuals to donate their tech.

Fix It

All devices gathered through our various sources are assessed for their condition. They are then refurbished through the replacing of components and thoroughly cleaned for the end-user. Devices unsuitable for use are scrapped for their components.

Donate It

Once a connection is made between SimplyEwaste and group/organization is made, we bring the devices to their destination. All devices donated are tested for their quality and usability. Anything left over will be recycled through a 3rd party.
